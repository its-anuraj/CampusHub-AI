## Script 3: Faculty & HOD Mentorship Desk (2 Mins)

* **Target Role:** 👩‍🏫 Faculty Members, Professors, and Heads of Departments (HODs)
* **Target Duration:** ~1 min 55 sec (~250 words)
* **Tone:** Educational, Empathetic, Mentor-Centric
* **On-Screen Visual:** Class Attendance roll-call, CIE marks grading sheet, and Parent Reachout card with preferred calling windows.

### 🎬 AI Voice Script:
> "Faculty members are the core of academic delivery and student mentorship. However, professors often spend valuable teaching hours on manual attendance roll-calls, paper grading, and managing difficult parent communications.
>
> CampusHub AI gives teachers a purpose-built Faculty Desk on web and mobile that automates routine chores so they can focus on teaching.
>
> First, our **Zero Cross-Exposure Security** guarantees that faculty members only see students enrolled in their assigned subjects. Taking class attendance takes under ten seconds with our 1-Tap Attendance Desk, which immediately highlights students falling below the mandatory 75% attendance threshold.
>
> Second, grading Continuous Internal Evaluations (CIE) is seamless. Teachers can input marks, analyze performance curves, and even use our built-in Bloom's Taxonomy AI engine to generate structured exam questions and evaluation rubrics automatically.
>
> But our most impactful innovation for teachers is the **Direct Parent Reachout Desk**. When a student struggles, teachers no longer have to dig through paper records for guardian numbers or risk calling parents at inconvenient times. Each student card displays verified guardian contact details alongside their designated preferred calling window — such as 'After 4 PM'. 
>
> This creates respectful, constructive collaboration between teachers and parents, driving student success with zero friction."
