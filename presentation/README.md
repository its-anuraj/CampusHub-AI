## 🚀 How to Generate the PPT in 2 Minutes:

1. Open **[Gamma.app](https://gamma.app)** or **[Tome.app](https://tome.app)**.
2. Click **"New from text / prompt"** (or *Generate Presentation*).
3. Open **[PPT_AI_PROMPT.md](file:///c:/Users/ajsin/OneDrive/Desktop/CampusHub_AI/presentation/PPT_AI_PROMPT.md)**, copy the entire prompt text, and paste it.
4. Select theme: **Dark Mode (Neon Indigo / Blue)**.
5. Hit **Generate** — your complete 14-slide presentation with diagrams and metrics will be ready!

## 🎙️ How to Generate AI Voiceover & Video Avatars:

1. Open **[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io)** (Voice) or **[HeyGen](https://heygen.com)** / **[D-ID](https://www.d-id.com)** (Video Avatar).
2. Choose your role script from **[ROLE_BASED_AI_VOICE_SCRIPTS.md](file:///c:/Users/ajsin/OneDrive/Desktop/CampusHub_AI/presentation/ROLE_BASED_AI_VOICE_SCRIPTS.md)**.
3. Paste the script text and generate studio-quality audio/video.
4. Overlay screen recordings of the **Web Portal** (`https://campushub-kcc.vercel.app`) and **Mobile App** for a presentation demo.
